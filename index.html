About Me

I am an undergraduate student in Fashion Innovation and Technology at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. My research is rooted in Smart and Interactive Textiles, with a core focus on Wearable Technology and Human-Computer Interaction.

My research philosophy centers on grounding interaction in genuine sensory experience. I treat textiles and fibers — from magnetically actuated structures to conductive architectures — as the interface itself, extending interaction beyond vision into touch and other underexplored senses. Looking forward, my goal is to develop wearable systems that are intelligent, adaptive, and effortless to use — technology that disappears into daily life rather than demanding attention from it.

Outside research, I'm drawn to photography, design, and AI-assisted art-making. I'm currently applying for research-based Master's / PhD programs for Fall 2027.

Projects

AuraFlow (Personal Project): A wearable anxiety-relief vest integrating HRV/ECG sensing, vibro-acoustic singing-bowl resonance, and airbag-driven haptic breathing guidance, powered in part by body-heat energy harvesting (TEG); paired with a companion app for AI-guided mindfulness modes and emergency alerts.
Living Silk: A magnetically actuated silk prototype, designed for interactive heritage-revitalization demonstrations in museum settings, that animates traditional Chinese embroidery motifs without embedding rigid electronics, developed with digitally embroidered magnetic-yarn architectures.
PatPatDots (HUFU): UI/UX and interaction design for an acupoint-tapping haptic vest with 12 independent haptic dots for gamified movement, reaction training, and somatic wellness, showcased at CES. View full project →
Wearable Electronics: Reconfigurable, detachable garment structures with laser-cut conductive-fabric components, focused on design detailing and embedded LED elements, with 10+ prototypes exhibited at the Global Sources Hong Kong Expo.
Droplets on Fibers: Physics, AI, and Art — studying droplet–fiber adhesion and fluid dynamics shaken in a martini glass, translated into a scientific visualization video submitted to the APS Division of Fluid Dynamics Gallery of Fluid Motion, and now extending into human-food interaction.
浮城 · Fauseng (Floating City): A narrative puzzle game exploring the language barriers faced by newcomers to Hong Kong, translating the experience of linguistic "aphasia" into pixel-art scenes and a Cantonese slang-decoding system built in Unity — awarded Second Prize at the Hong Kong Digital Art Competition (HKDADC).
The Last Human on Earth, We Recorded His Day: An AI-generated short film for the Bilibili AI Creation Competition (Open Track), following the last human alive and the androids who cared for him across two centuries. Contributed art direction and AI video generation for the film's visual world. View full project →
Memora (Personal Project): A service system for old-clothes recycling and emotional memorialization — combining a community recycling exchange, a keepsake-making process, and an AR memorial layer that lets people revisit a fragment of meaning after letting a garment go.
Rehabilitation Shoulder Brace (Mengma): Product appearance design and material testing for a sports rehabilitation shoulder brace, from digital prototyping to final iteration.
